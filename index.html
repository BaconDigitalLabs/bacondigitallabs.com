The gap isn't your team. It's the handoffs.

Calls, missed calls, website forms, booking tools, email, and referrals all lead somewhere different. The software is usually fine on its own—the handoffs between systems are where ownership, response, and follow-up get hard to see.

Website & forms

CRM & field service tools

Good platforms, underused. Native routing, aging, and alerting features often sit unconfigured.

What the sprint covers

In two weeks, we map, test, and harden one lead path end to end—using your existing tools wherever practical. Custom code is used only where a real gap is confirmed.

In scope

  • Map one inquiry path from customer to your system of record
  • Verify website/form, email, phone, and CRM/FSM handoffs
  • Configure native features you already pay for
  • Add a consent-aware acknowledgment for the selected path
  • Make ownership and aging visible
  • Add retry, exception, and alert behavior where an integration is required
  • Test valid, duplicate, failure, no-consent, and aging cases
  • Deliver a runbook, test evidence, and a 30-day results review

What you can see afterward

  • Which source produced the inquiry
  • When it reached the operating system
  • Whether an approved acknowledgment was attempted
  • Who or what queue owns the next action
  • Which leads or estimates are aging without action
  • Which events failed, and who was alerted

How it works

  1. 01

    Baseline & map

    A short owner/operations discovery, an inventory of your website, forms, phone, inbox, calendar, and CRM/FSM tools, and a written pilot boundary before anything changes.

  2. 02

    Repair & connect

    Fix routing defects on the selected path, configure native CRM/FSM features first, add an acknowledgment where consent allows it, and add retry and exception handling to any required integration.

  3. 03

    Prove & hand off

    Run controlled test cases, confirm duplicate and failure handling, deliver a runbook and access inventory, and train your team in 30 minutes.
